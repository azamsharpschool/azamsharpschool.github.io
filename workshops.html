FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need AI experience?

No AI experience is required for the first workshop. Basic Swift and SwiftUI knowledge is recommended.

Do I need an Apple Intelligence device?

Yes. To follow along on a device, you'll need a compatible iPhone or iPad with Apple Intelligence enabled and the on-device model downloaded.

Where are the workshops hosted?

Both workshops are delivered live through Zoom. Registration is handled through Luma.

Will source code be provided?

Yes. The workshop project source code is included with registration.

Can I attend both workshops?

Yes. Each workshop is a separate event. You can register for either workshop or attend both.
